How to read this direction

Positive and negative describe the northward or southward contribution of the recorded pattern. They are not ratings of your health.

“With Pressure” means the eastward Recovery component calculated from available sleep and stress readings. Its influence relative to the north/south contribution sets the angle.

At East, the net north/south contribution is at the pivot while Recovery pressure remains. This is not an ideal state. The pivot can result from offsetting contributions or no net north/south contribution; it does not prove every card is in conflict.

  • North — Positive Direction
  • North-East — Positive, With Pressure
  • East — Mixed Signals
  • South-East — Negative, With Pressure
  • South — Negative Direction

The cards explain which evidence is present and how each signal contributes. A change toward East does not, by itself, prove that Recovery pressure increased.

Response temporarily uses its previous calculation while Compass V2 is introduced. The two may show different directions.
