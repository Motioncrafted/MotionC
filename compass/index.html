          How it’s calculated

          Each driver creates a configurable contribution to a two-dimensional vector. North is positive Y, South is negative Y, East is positive X and West is negative X. Missing information contributes nothing—it never counts as Balanced.

          The result is an interpretation of recorded behaviour and trends. It does not measure real-time fuel use, insulin, metabolic flexibility or other unrecorded physiology.
